# Key Visual Prompt

```text
A cinematic dark fantasy pixel-art key visual for an original indie mobile action roguelite game, 16:9 wide banner.

A lone small Mourning Warden stands at the center of a cursed moonlit grave field, holding a warm golden funeral lantern. Around the hero, five distinct faction silhouettes form a threatening semicircle: ghostly choir figures with bells, thorn-crowned plant nobles, ash-armored knights, masked dead merchants with coin charms, and low moonless beasts with glowing eyes.

Above them, a pale moon shines through deep navy clouds, and luminous runic ledger marks spread across the sky like a magical record of the player's past actions. On the ground around the hero are symbolic traces of previous battles: a broken sword, a poisonous flower, scattered ash, old coins, and black thorns.

The image should communicate that the world remembers the player's actions. Mysterious, heroic, tense but not horror. Strong central silhouette, readable at small mobile-store thumbnail size. Rich night palette: deep navy, violet shadows, pale moonlight blue, warm funeral gold, muted emerald and ember red accents.

High-resolution polished indie game key art, detailed 16-bit inspired pixel art, crisp pixel edges, cinematic composition. No text, no logo, no UI, no copyrighted characters, no imitation of existing games.
```

## Negative/Constraint Notes

- No logo.
- No UI.
- No text.
- No copyrighted characters.
- Do not imitate Shadow of Mordor, Lord of the Rings, or any existing IP.
- Keep the hero readable at mobile store thumbnail size.
